# Privacy Policy
*Last Updated: April 21, 2026*

## 1. Introduction
Gene Setu ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and services.

## 2. Information We Collect
We collect information that you provide directly to us when you create an account, use our health tracking features, or communicate with us.

### 2.1 Health Connect Data (Android)
Our app requests access to Google Health Connect to provide advanced biological insights. We strictly adhere to the [Health Connect Permissions Policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13148421).

We access the following read-only data types from Health Connect:
*   **Heart Rate & Resting Heart Rate:** To calculate cardiac efficiency and recovery scores.
*   **Heart Rate Variability (HRV):** To monitor autonomic nervous system status and biological stress levels.
*   **Step Count & Distance:** To analyze physical activity patterns and calculate movement efficiency.
*   **Sleep Data:** To evaluate recovery quality and its impact on biological readiness.
*   **Oxygen Saturation (SpO2) & Respiratory Rate:** To detect early warning signals of physiological strain.
*   **Weight:** To track body composition trends and correlate with metabolic health markers.
*   **Exercise Sessions:** To provide context for cardiovascular load and training intensity.

**Data Usage & Retention:**
*   **Minimum Scope:** We only request access to data types essential for our "Bio-Report" and health analysis features.
*   **Local Processing:** Much of your health data is processed locally on your device to generate insights.
*   **Prohibited Use:** We do NOT sell your health data to third parties, advertisers, or data brokers. We do NOT use health data for credit-granting or insurance underwriting.

## 3. How We Use Your Information
We use the information we collect to:
*   Provide and maintain our health tracking services.
*   Generate personalized "Bio-Reports" and biological scores.
*   Calculate "Recovery & Readiness" metrics.
*   Provide personalized daily health advice based on your biometric trends.
*   Notify you of potential early warning signals in your biological data.

## 4. Data Sharing and Disclosure
We do not share your personal health data with third parties except:
*   With your explicit consent.
*   To comply with legal obligations.
*   To protect our rights and safety.

## 5. Data Security
We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

## 6. Your Rights
Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. You can disconnect Health Connect access at any time through the app settings or the Android system settings.
